## Day 5

Day 5 was just a chill day, went to Goodwill, saw some mildly weird stuff (but not much, the conclusion I drew is that
American Goodwills are just kinda lame compared to our Canadian ones), then went to Target for snacks and stuff. We got
a box of "Simply NKD" chips, which I had never seen before (and I don't think we've gotten them yet up here in Canada),
but they were pretty yummy! They're pretty much just the usual chips, just without the artificial flavours or any dye.
My best comparison is for the Doritos; They're like if you took normal tortilla chips and simply added the flavouring of
normal Doritos.

## Day 7

The 4th of July rolls around, and it's the last full day of the trip. We just kinda hang out for the first bit of it,
then later in the day we had a photoshoot session! For New Years earlier this year when I was over, I got my boyfriend
to pose for a few funny photos, so this time we did some more proper ones! I'm not going to include them without asking
(as cute as some of them were :3), but it was Day 7's main event I'd say! After that, we waited for it to get dark so we
could sit out back at the fire table and relax. With lots of fireworks going off in the background we made some s'mores,
cuddled, and just had a nice time. It was an amazing end to the trip and definitely helped with the sadness that was
inevitably going to happen.
