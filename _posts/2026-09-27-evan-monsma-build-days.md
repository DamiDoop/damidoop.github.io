So my favourite Youtuber, Evan Monsma, has this thing he does in October called "Build Days" and I want to try and participate in my own little way. He explains in his video that there aren't really any solid rules, and it's more so to help motivate people to create whatever it is they enjoy creating every day of the month. Keeping with the chill theme, you don't *have* to create something if you're not feeling it or you're busy, and you can just work on something, even if you can't complete it.

This chill sort of ethos of his is one of the many reasons he's my favourite Youtuber, and why I want to take part in Build Days. If you know me, or have read some of my last blog posts, you know that I do photography as a hobby, which is what I'm going to try and do the most of this month. I'm also going to try to write a blog post detailing what I did each day, even if it's just a collection of photos.

That's all I have for now, see you all October 1st!
