Yep. I am conciously deciding to name my blog post that, and welcome to day 8!

Today I took the time to finally set up [netboot.xyz](https://netboot.xyz) on my main server and router. I have looked into it in the past but have always stopped because it was too complicated for me. Today though, I felt more up to the task for some reason. One of the things I struggled with was understanding the best way to do the DNS and DHCP magic that it wants you to set up, but today I realised that because my router now just runs OpenWRT (which is just Linux!), it was going to be fairly simple.

With this realisation, I went to the docs aaaaand was completely unsure about what parst of their example `dnsmasq.conf` I needed to adapt for my setup. As it turns out, according to [this post](https://forum.openwrt.org/t/dnsmasq-pxe-boot-using-netboot-xyz/56075/2) on the OpenWRT forum, all I had to do was ommit some things. Pretty easy!

I immediately went and tested it and with only some very mild surprise, it just worked. I have so far run into only a handful of issues. One being that some of the options just.. don't work. For example, the Fedora live options do not work at all, and Debian 13 live will download the kernel and initramfs, boot, then fail to download/mount the squashfs on real hardware, then my other issue is probably just issues with pxe on my laptops themselves (slow or fail to get an IP through DHCP for some reason).

I did manage to install an operating system through this though, that being OpenBSD on my ThinkPad T480. I would've preferred FreeBSD, but I have no idea why it wasn't showing up despite the fact it exists in the config.

Beyond getting netboot.xyz set up, my main desktop continues to explode and have weird issues that make me scared to keep using it. So I suppose for every cool computer thing I figure out or fix, my desktop will suffer. Perhaps that Dell Precision should've replaced it for the time being rather than being server #2.

Anyways, that's all from me today and I promise I'll get out and shooting eventually. See you next time!
